# Operations — project, flow, march (and certify)

> Three operations move a configuration with respect to the conditions, and they are **one geometric
> object seen three ways**: the corrector onto a submanifold, the gradient descent *along* it, and
> the continuation that *tracks* it. All three are generated by the held constraints' Jacobian.
> Code: `src/solvers/`, `src/search/certify.ts`.

## The hub: the constraint Jacobian

At a configuration on the submanifold `M = ⋂{gᵢ = 0}`, stack the held constraints' Jacobian `J`.
One damped least-squares solve with `G = JJᵀ` gives the entire local geometry:

- the **corrector** (reach `M`): the minimum-norm Gauss–Newton step `−Jᵀ(JJᵀ)⁻¹ g`;
- the **tangent projector** (move *along* `M`): `P_T v = v − Jᵀ(JJᵀ)⁻¹ J v`, the projection onto
  the tangent space `T_pM = ker J`.

So `project` and `flow` are not unrelated solvers — they are the corrector and the tangent-descent of
the *same* object. `tangentProject` and `project` share the same damped `JJᵀ` solve. Everything runs
in a [chart](configuration.md)'s coordinates `X`: residuals are evaluated on `ι(x)`, derivatives
pulled back through `Dι`. The solvers are **problem-agnostic** — they take the abstract `Constraint`
(a `Fn` from `functions/`, optionally a `Held` saying how to use it) / `Chart` / `Energy` / `Region`
contracts, never a `Triangulation` (the torus rides inside the maps' closures).

## project — land on the submanifold

`project(chart, x, held)`: minimum-norm Gauss–Newton onto `⋂{gᵢ = 0}`. The corrector above, iterated
to convergence. Damping `λ` makes a rank-deficient `J` (redundant constraints) harmless. This is the
generalization of the old `newtonFlatten` (it reproduces it on `[flat]`, and `semiSolutionFlatten` on
`[flat, collinear, collinear]` in a pinned chart).

## flow — gradient descent *along* the submanifold, into a region

`flow(chart, x, held, energy, {region?})`: **Riemannian** gradient descent of an `Energy` `E` on `M`.
Per step: `g = ∇E`, tangent-project `g_T = P_T g`, step `x -= α g_T` (purely along `M`), then
`project` to retract the O(α²) curvature drift. With a `region`, the step is a backtracking line
search that only accepts a retracted point inside `Ω` that lowers `E` — else it halts `blocked`.

This is the honest gradient flow on `M`: we move along the manifold, not in the ambient direction.
(The old `embeddedFlow` stepped the full ambient gradient — normal component and all — then
re-flattened; that perturbs *which* point of `M` you reach.) It is the tool to descend *into* the
tiny region `Ω`. The gate is essential: un-gated repulsion descent can leave `Ω` (because
`minMargin>0 ≠ isEmbedded`); the gate enforces the topological truth.

## march — continuation that *tracks* manifold ∩ region

`march(chart, x, family, target, {region})`: walk a 1-parameter family of submanifolds `M_s` to a
target value, staying inside `Ω`. This is the tool for a target one projection can't hit — `M_s` far,
`Ω` tiny. It is pure corrector-continuation: advance the parameter by a `step`, `project` onto `M_s`
(rebuilt at the current point, so frozen charts re-freeze each substep), re-gate `Ω`; grow `step` on
success, halve on failure. When feasible steps shrink to nothing the path is **pinched** — `march`
returns `blocked` with the value reached (a *result*: where `Ω` closes, e.g. a margin going to zero
as you approach a wall — not an error). No bespoke numerics: it reuses `project` verbatim.

The three together answer the project's goal — *flat (and fixed modulus) inside embedded*: `project`
lands on `M`, `flow` descends into `Ω`, `march` tracks `M_s ∩ Ω` as the modulus target moves.

## certify — record the result

`certify(torus, positions) → Certificate`: the standard measurement of "what torus is this" — cone
deficit, embeddedness, margin, and **both** moduli: raw `τ` (Teichmüller) *and* reduced `τ̂` (moduli).
Keeping both prevents the recurring mistake of storing only the reduced point and later reading it as
Teichmüller. Every search ends here.

## In code

| symbol | file | role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `project` | `solvers/project.ts` | min-norm Gauss–Newton corrector onto `⋂{g=0}` |
| `tangentProject` | `solvers/tangentProject.ts` | `P_T v = v − Jᵀ(JJᵀ)⁻¹Jv` (the shared tangent solve) |
| `flow` | `solvers/flow.ts` | Riemannian descent of an energy on `M`, gated by a region |
| `march`, `Family` | `solvers/march.ts` | continuation tracking `M_s ∩ Ω` to a target; reports `blocked` at a pinch |
| `certify`, `Certificate` | `search/certify.ts` | the recorded measurement (raw τ AND reduced τ̂, margin, embedded) |

Correctness is checked against **known math** — toy manifolds with closed-form answers (a linear
energy on a sphere → the antipode; a point tracked along a circle with a closed-form pinch) and the
realized geometry of the tori — not against reproducing the old solvers. Pure: no three.js, no DOM.
