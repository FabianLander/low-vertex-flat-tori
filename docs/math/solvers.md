# Operations — project, flow, march (and certify)

> Three operations move a configuration with respect to the conditions, and they are **one geometric
> object seen three ways**: the corrector onto a submanifold, the gradient descent *along* it, and
> the continuation that *tracks* it. All three are generated by the held constraints' Jacobian, and all
> run **entirely on the problem's space ℝⁿ**. Code: `src/solvers/`, `src/search/certify.ts`.

## The hub: the constraint Jacobian

At a configuration on the submanifold `M = ⋂{gᵢ = 0}`, stack the held constraints' Jacobian `J`.
One damped least-squares solve with `G = JJᵀ` gives the entire local geometry:

- the **corrector** (reach `M`): the minimum-norm Gauss–Newton step `−Jᵀ(JJᵀ)⁻¹ g`;
- the **tangent projector** (move *along* `M`): `P_T v = v − Jᵀ(JJᵀ)⁻¹ J v`, the projection onto
  the tangent space `T_pM = ker J`.

So `project` and `flow` are not unrelated solvers — they are the corrector and the tangent-descent of
the *same* object. `tangentProject` and `project` share the same damped `JJᵀ` solve. Everything runs
**directly on ℝⁿ**: the constraints are `Fn`s already *pulled* into the working space through a
coordinate system's embedding (see [configuration-space.md](configuration-space.md)), so their
Jacobians are in ℝⁿ and there is no pullback step inside the solver. The solvers are
**problem-agnostic** — they take a `Constraint` (an `Fn`, optionally a `Held` saying which rows to
drive), a `ScalarFn` (energy), and a `Gate` predicate — never a `Triangulation`, an `Embedding`, or a
chart. The min-norm step is taken in the working space's Euclidean metric (`g = I`); the canonical,
reparameterization-invariant choice is the pullback metric `DφᵀDφ`, a documented, deferred seam (it
coincides with `I` for every current coordinate system).

## project — land on the submanifold

`project(x, held)`: minimum-norm Gauss–Newton onto `⋂{gᵢ = 0}`, the corrector above iterated to
convergence. Damping `λ` makes a rank-deficient `J` (redundant constraints) harmless. Pinning some
coordinates or imposing a symmetry is **not** a solver flag — it is the choice of coordinate system the
constraints were pulled through (e.g. `pinCoords`), so `project` itself never special-cases it. A
constraint that knows its rank drops its redundant driving rows (e.g. `flat` drops the Gauss–Bonnet
row → full-rank `G`); `λ` carries any residual rank-deficiency.

## flow — gradient descent *along* the submanifold, into a region

`flow(x, held, energy, {gate?})`: **Riemannian** gradient descent of a scalar `Fn` energy `E` on `M`.
Per step: `g = ∇E`, tangent-project `g_T = P_T g`, step `x -= α g_T` (purely along `M`), then `project`
to retract the O(α²) curvature drift. With a `gate` (the embedded region's `contains`, pulled to an ℝⁿ
predicate), the step is a backtracking line search that accepts only a retracted point passing the gate
that also lowers `E`; else it halts `blocked`. The adaptive backtracking is what lets a stiff energy (a
log-barrier whose gradient blows up near contact) be descended without overshoot — no separate
step-normalization knob.

This is the honest gradient flow on `M`: we move along the manifold, not in the ambient direction, and
the retraction only cleans up curvature drift. It is the tool to descend *into* the tiny region `Ω`.
The gate is essential: un-gated repulsion descent can leave `Ω` (because `minMargin > 0 ≠ isEmbedded`);
the gate enforces the topological truth.

## march — continuation that *tracks* manifold ∩ region

`march(x, family, target, {gate})`: walk a 1-parameter family of submanifolds `M_s` to a target value,
staying inside `Ω`. The tool for a target one projection can't hit — `M_s` far, `Ω` tiny. Pure
corrector-continuation: advance the parameter by a `step`, `project` onto `M_s` (rebuilt at the current
point, so frozen charts re-freeze each substep), re-gate `Ω`; grow `step` on success, halve on failure.
The `Family` is ℝⁿ-facing — `param(x)` reads the marched coordinate (pushing to ℝ³ⱽ internally for an
ambient quantity like the modulus), `held(x, s)` rebuilds the constraints already pulled into ℝⁿ. When
feasible steps shrink to nothing the path is **pinched** — `march` returns `blocked` with the value
reached (a *result*: where `Ω` closes, not an error). No bespoke numerics: it reuses `project` verbatim.

The three together answer the project's goal — *flat (and fixed modulus) inside embedded*: `project`
lands on `M`, `flow` descends into `Ω`, `march` tracks `M_s ∩ Ω` as the modulus target moves.

## certify — record the result

`certify(torus, positions) → Certificate`: the standard measurement of "what torus is this" — cone
deficit, embeddedness, margin, and **both** moduli: raw `τ` (Teichmüller) *and* reduced `τ̂` (moduli).
Keeping both prevents the recurring mistake of storing only the reduced point and later reading it as
Teichmüller. Every search ends here.

## In code

| symbol | file | role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `project` | `solvers/project.ts` | min-norm Gauss–Newton corrector onto `⋂{g=0}`, on ℝⁿ |
| `tangentProject` | `solvers/tangentProject.ts` | `P_T v = v − Jᵀ(JJᵀ)⁻¹Jv` (the shared tangent solve) |
| `flow` | `solvers/flow.ts` | Riemannian descent of an energy on `M`, gated by a `Gate` |
| `march`, `Family` | `solvers/march.ts` | continuation tracking `M_s ∩ Ω`; reports `blocked` at a pinch |
| `Gate` | `solvers/types.ts` | the open region as the solver sees it: a predicate on ℝⁿ |
| `Constraint`, `Held`, `Region` | `conditions/types.ts` | the condition contracts the solvers consume |
| `pull*` | `search/pull.ts` | pull a `Constraint`/`Region` into a coordinate system (→ ℝⁿ `Fn`/`Gate`) |
| `certify`, `Certificate` | `search/certify.ts` | the recorded measurement (raw τ AND reduced τ̂, margin, embedded) |

Correctness is checked against **known math** — toy manifolds with closed-form answers (a linear
energy on a sphere → the antipode; a point tracked along a circle with a closed-form pinch) and the
realized geometry of the tori. Because the solvers run on bare ℝⁿ, a toy test *is* just ℝⁿ with some
functions and a predicate — no triangulation, no coordinate system. Pure: no three.js, no DOM.
